## 5. Duplicate-Record Assessment

Duplicate analysis should distinguish between:

- Exact duplicate records
- Repeated country-year combinations
- Different capacity indicators for the same country and year
- Separate geographic aggregates
- Legitimate source revisions

A repeated country-year combination is not necessarily an erroneous duplicate when additional dimensions distinguish the observations.

**Measured duplicate count:** Pending validation.

## 9. Known Limitations

- Historical statistics may be revised.
- Geographic reporting coverage may vary.
- Capacity values do not represent actual electricity generation.
- Installed capacity does not directly indicate plant utilization.
- Missing observations do not necessarily represent zero capacity.
- Data may not reflect recent installations or retirements.
- No independently measured overall quality score is claimed.
